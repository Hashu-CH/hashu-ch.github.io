Randomized procedural tracks built for use by [WheeledLab](https://github.com/UWRobotLearning/WheeledLab) to train racing policies on closed loop tracks or on variable difficulty Bezier chains.

I ended up abstracting many of the difficulty 'knobs' to a single difficulty parameter, but it's well documented enough that anyone can make finer adjustments to track generation.

- [Source](https://github.com/Hashu-CH/procedural-track-generator)
